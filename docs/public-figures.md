# Public figures the reports set beside their own

Read on 2026-10-17. Each quotation below is copied verbatim from the page named in the `Source:` line above it,
its typographic apostrophes and its no-break space before "MW" included (a network test of
`tests/test_soundness.py` checks it on the page). A
figure here is not a run's number and decides nothing: the validation report prints it beside the soundness section's
measured slopes (section 3.1, added after the freeze), with how it is defined, so that the comparison is said to be
like for like or not. `src/valiwatt/report/soundness.py` (`PUBLIC_FIGURE`) holds the same quotation, and a test holds
the two together.

## Québec's demand sensitivity to temperature

Radio-Canada, Thomas Gerbet, « Grand froid : le jour où Hydro-Québec a atteint sa pointe de frayeur », published on
2025-01-20, quoting Hydro-Québec's spokesperson Caroline Des Rosiers.

Source: <https://ici.radio-canada.ca/nouvelle/2133488/froid-pointe-hydro-quebec-frayeur-record-postes-documents>

> Une variation d’un degré Celsius représente 500 MW d’impact sur la charge à alimenter, dit la porte-parole de la
> société d'État, Caroline Des Rosiers.

How it is defined: the article gives none of it. It names no temperature the degree is a change of (an hour's, a day's
mean, a station's), no hours (the winter peak is the article's subject), no year, and does not say whether the
demand of the day before is allowed for. It is a spokesperson's round figure, reported by a newspaper, not a published
estimate. The comparison with the model's slopes is therefore not like for like. The web searches of 2026-10-17 found no
figure in MW per °C in a Hydro-Québec publication or a Régie de l'énergie filing; those documents (the 2023-2032
supply plan among them) were not read in full, so one may still hold such a figure.
